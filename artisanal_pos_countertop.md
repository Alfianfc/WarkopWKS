## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, tactile, and fast-paced operational interface tailored for modern artisanal cafes, specialty coffee roasters, and elevated "warkop" counter culture. The design narrative rejects digital clutter and generic POS aesthetics in favor of a clean, editorial, and utility-driven architectural presence. It honors the craft of the barista and cashier: brisk gestures, tactile responsiveness, zero cognitive drag, and unflinching clarity beneath warm cafe lighting.

The aesthetic fuses contemporary Swiss minimalism with warm organic hospitality. Visual discipline is maintained through architectural precision, strict vertical and horizontal alignments, rich organic contrasts (deep espresso against fresh linen whites), and energetic terracotta triggers for decisive commercial actions (tenders, additions, charge completions). The interface feels durable, effortless, and premium—evoking the sensory tactility of heavyweight ceramic cups, oiled walnut counters, and polished chrome portafilters.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the physical materials of specialty coffee craft: steam, raw ceramics, dark roast beans, and energetic terracotta signage.

### Primary Role (Action & State Triggers)
- **Primary Main (`#E05A2B`)**: Terracotta orange. Dedicated exclusively to conversion points, charge triggers, active order indicators, and critical touch targets.
- **Primary Hover/Active (`#C8491E`)**: Deeper roast terracotta for active press states.
- **Primary Accent / Light (`#FFEDE6`)**: Warm wash container used for active selection states, order tags, and table statuses.

### Secondary Role (Grounding & Typography)
- **Espresso Obsidian (`#2D1F18`)**: Deepest mocha-tinted black for primary typographic hierarchy, receipts, key navigation active pills, and high-impact structural panels.
- **Medium Roast (`#4A3528`)**: Secondary structural elements, column borders on dark components, and subheadings.
- **Mocha Tan (`#785A46`)**: De-emphasized details, timestamps, cashier attribution, and inactive icons.

### Tertiary Role (Atmosphere & Warm Fill)
- **Steamed Cream (`#F4EBE1`)**: Latte foam tint for table groupings, receipt background surfaces, and secondary button fills.
- **Toasted Oat (`#E8DFD5`)**: Active border accents on soft panels and divider accents.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Base layer for order registers, item cards, and active modals.
- **Stone White (`#FAFAF9`)**: Background sheet canvas for the mobile application chassis.
- **Countertop Stone (`#F5F5F4`)**: Recessed input containers, empty states, and inactive tab tracks.
- **Crisp Border Hairline (`#E7E5E4`)**: Non-negotiable structural 1px delineators that frame cards, grids, and tickets without muddiness.
- **Functional Statuses**: Success (`#15803D`), Alert (`#B91C1C`), Pending Kitchen (`#D97706`).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans powers the entire typographic architecture. Its geometric clarity combined with subtle humanist apertures provides maximum readability during rapid cashier scanning under variable screen glare.

- **Monetary Figures & Currency**: Numbers must always be styled with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during rapid cart accumulation or split-bill calculation.
- **Weight Strategy**: Headings use `600` and `700` to anchor line items and category segments against dense item grids. Body text utilizes `400` for secondary metadata (bean origin, milk variant) and `500` for primary menu titles. Labels use `600` or `700` uppercase micro-tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`) for status tags, table indicators, and modifiers.
- **Line Heights**: Kept snug to prevent order tickets and line items from sprawling unnecessarily on small-screen handhelds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 4px baseline sub-grid, scaling in 8px steps for primary bounding boxes. Because speed and finger accuracy determine the usability of a counter POS on mobile viewports, ergonomics dictate spacing constraints:

- **Touch Bounds**: Every clickable checkout action, product cell, modifier incrementor, and category tab has a non-negotiable minimum hit area of 44px (`2.75rem`), insulated by at least 8px (`space-xs`) outer clearance.
- **Mobile Viewport Grid**: Mobile views utilize a fluid 4-column column system with 16px (`space-md`) horizontal page margins and 12px (`space-sm`) gutters. Menu arrays display as a 2-column card matrix on phones and scale to a 3-to-4-column responsive fluid grid on small tablets/counter displays.
- **Docked Action Trays**: Order totals and payment trigger bars must remain permanently fixed to the bottom viewport boundary, wrapped in a high-contrast container with safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 16px`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using crisp borders paired with warm, diffuse ambient occlusion to convey depth without dirtiness.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FAFAF9` baseline background without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Product Tiles)**: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a 1px solid border in `#E7E5E4` and an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(45, 31, 24, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(45, 31, 24, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Sticky Footer Bar, Modifiers Sheet)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E7E5E4` and elevated shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(45, 31, 24, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(45, 31, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Payment Bottom-Sheets)**: Backdropped with a semi-opaque scrim (`rgba(45, 31, 24, 0.4)`), lifted by `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(45, 31, 24, 0.16)`.
- **Pressed States**: Active touches compress down cleanly, shifting the border to `#785A46` and dropping shadow elevation to zero, providing physical button feedback.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a refined, friendly architectural radius:
- **Base Cards & Menu Panels**: 12px to 16px radius (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`).
- **Primary Buttons & Action Panels**: 12px radius, yielding an inviting, confident touch geometry without becoming bubbly or juvenile.
- **Chips, Category Pills, & Status Badges**: Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full` or 9999px) to distinctly contrast against rectangular product cards.
- **Steppers & Quantity Modifiers**: 8px (`rounded-sm`) to lock in industrial precision for counter tallies.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary Action (Charge / Pay)**: Solid `#E05A2B` fill, `#FFFFFF` bold text, zero border, 12px corner radius. Minimum height 52px on mobile for blind thumb execution.
- **Secondary (Custom Note, Hold Order)**: Cream background `#F4EBE1`, text in `#2D1F18`, hairline border `#E8DFD5`.
- **Destructive (Void / Cancel)**: Soft white with `#B91C1C` label and subtle `#FEE2E2` active state.

### Category Chips & Filters
- **Default State**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E7E5E4`, text `#4A3528`.
- **Active State**: Solid `#2D1F18` fill, crisp white typography, high contrast, immediate visual focus.

### Product / Menu Card
- Clean white tile with crisp 1px border `#E7E5E4` and 12px corner radius. 
- Image at the top with a subtle 4:3 aspect ratio, framed by a soft `#F5F5F4` thumbnail placeholder.
- Title in `Plus Jakarta Sans 600` (#2D1F18), price highlighted beneath in tabular lining numbers using deep espresso (#2D1F18).
- Quick-add floating trigger (+) located bottom-right, equipped with a dedicated 36px hit target.

### Order Cart Item / Ticket Row
- Single-line or two-line dense format with clean 1px bottom divider (`#F5F5F4`).
- Left: Quantity pill badge (`#F4EBE1` fill with `#2D1F18` text).
- Center: Item title with condensed modifier list underneath (e.g., "+ Oat Milk, Less Sugar" in `#785A46`).
- Right: Tabular price tally with an inline swipe-to-remove or tap-to-edit action.

### Stepper & Quantity Controller
- Segmented container with `#F5F5F4` fill and 8px corners.
- Minus and Plus operations flanked at the edges with 36px square thumb spaces.
- Numerical value centered in tabular bold typography.

### Input Fields & Keypads
- **Form Inputs**: Background `#FAFAF9`, 1px solid border `#E7E5E4`, focused border 1.5px `#E05A2B` with zero fuzzy focus rings.
- **Quick Numpad for Cash Tender**: Flush grid cells, 60px minimum height per digit, crisp dividers, high-contrast numerals (`22px 600`).
